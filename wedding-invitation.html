Our Story

A Timeline Nobody Requested

"Ten years. One corgi. Zero regrets (probably)."

We met at university — back when the biggest decisions were which lectures to skip and which instant noodles to buy. It was not love at first sight. Christy made good decisions. Mitchell made Christy's decisions slightly more interesting.

What followed was a decade of building a life together — new cities, new jobs, one very opinionated corgi — held together by the kind of love that doesn't announce itself loudly, but simply refuses to leave.

We grew up, more or less. We won't pretend every day was poetic. Some days were very much about whose turn it was to do the dishes. But we kept showing up for each other, through the mundane and the meaningful, and somewhere along the way it became the best thing either of us ever accidentally did.

After a decade of practice runs, we decided to make it permanent. This is that wedding. You're invited. Please come — we've already paid for your dinner.
